## 🕒 Tiempos Presentes

| Tiempo Verbal | Estructura | Uso Principal | Ejemplo | Palabras Clave |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Present Simple** | `Sujeto + V (s/es)` | Rutinas, verdades generales o hábitos. | *I play football every Saturday.* | always, usually, often, every day |
| **Present Continuous** | `Sujeto + am/is/are + V-ing` | Acciones que ocurren en el momento. | *She is reading a book right now.* | now, right now, at the moment |
| **Present Perfect** | `Sujeto + have/has + V-V3 (Pasado Participio)` | Experiencias o acciones pasadas con relevancia actual. | *They have lived here for 5 years.* | already, yet, just, ever, never, since, for |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | `Sujeto + have/has been + V-ing` | Acciones iniciadas en el pasado que continúan hoy. | *He has been working all morning.* | for, since, all day, lately |

## ⏪ Tiempos Pasados

| Tiempo Verbal | Estructura | Uso Principal | Ejemplo | Palabras Clave |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Past Simple** | `Sujeto + V-V2 (Pasado Simple)` | Acciones completadas en un tiempo específico del pasado. | *I visited Paris last year.* | yesterday, last week, in 2010, ago |
| **Past Continuous** | `Sujeto + was/were + V-ing` | Acciones en progreso en un momento concreto del pasado. | *I was studying when you called.* | while, when, at that time |
| **Past Perfect** | `Sujeto + had + V-V3 (Pasado Participio)` | Acción pasada ocurrida antes de otra acción pasada. | *She had left before I arrived.* | before, after, by the time, already |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | `Sujeto + had been + V-ing` | Acción prolongada en progreso antes de otro punto del pasado. | *They had been waiting for two hours.* | for, since, before, how long |

## 🚀 Tiempos Futuros

| Tiempo Verbal | Estructura | Uso Principal | Ejemplo | Palabras Clave |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Future Simple (Will)** | `Sujeto + will + V-base` | Decisiones espontáneas, promesas o predicciones sin evidencia. | *I will help you with that.* | tomorrow, next week, probably, I think |
| **Be Going To** | `Sujeto + am/is/are going to + V-base` | Planes, intenciones previas o predicciones con evidencia directa. | *Look at those clouds! It is going to rain.* | planned, intended, look at... |
| **Future Continuous** | `Sujeto + will be + V-ing` | Acciones que estarán en progreso en un momento futuro. | *This time tomorrow, I will be flying.* | at 8 PM tomorrow, this time next week |
| **Future Perfect** | `Sujeto + will have + V-V3 (Pasado Participio)` | Acciones que habrán finalizado antes de un punto futuro. | *By 2028, I will have finished my degree.* | by, by the time, by next year |
| **Future Perfect Continuous** | `Sujeto + will have been + V-ing` | Duración de una acción prolongada hasta un momento futuro. | *By 5 PM, she will have been teaching for 4 hours.* | by..., for... |

---

## 💡 Resumen Estructural Rápido

* **Simple:** Enfocado en el hecho o rutina en sí (`V / V-ed / Will V`).
* **Continuous / Progressive:** Enfocado en la duración o acción en progreso (`Be + V-ing`).
* **Perfect:** Conecta dos puntos en el tiempo o indica completitud (`Have + V3`).
* **Perfect Continuous:** Enfatiza la duración acumulada hasta un punto específico (`Have been + V-ing`).
